# Retired public evidence reader

The separate `chopper-evidence.pages.dev` reader and its source application were retired on 2026-11-17. It is separate from the [Chopper hosted app](https://chopper-1rx.pages.dev/), which remains the browser product.

The reader source, its license and all four published objects were copied to the approved private archive. An independent authenticated reader verified the archive and every member before the Pages project was deleted. R2 data was retained; deleting the reader did not delete evidence objects or private originals.

## Preserved evidence

The archive retains the two small benchmark originals, the deterministic 32 MiB download fixture and its recipe, and the reviewed E5 public derivative. Historical links and source IDs describe that earlier publication; they no longer promise a live public download. Exact historical originals remain subject to the [evidence access boundary](../evidence-storage.md).

The reader's exact source TAR has SHA-256 `e4c3ee54c6e79bb292b1d6163047c226017eb44937c118c2cf675e2857041901`. Historical measurements and failed attempts were not edited or recertified by this retirement.
